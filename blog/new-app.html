My Idea for a New App

10-8-25

I have an idea for a new app. It is a tagging system for songs that lets you create temporary playlists based on your mood. You would start by searching through songs and tagging them with whatever tag you want, such as happy, mellow, raining, literally whatever you want. Then, you would go to the creation tab and choose which tags you want to add to the playlist. Finally, you would press play and it would create that playlist in your Spotify account and send you straight to it. If you see this and would actually use the app let me know because I have no idea if it would be useful for more people than just me.
